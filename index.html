When is the personal income tax deadline in Canada?

For most individuals, the T1 return is due April 30. If you or your spouse or common-law partner are self-employed, the filing deadline is June 15, but any balance owing is still due April 30.

Source: CRA — Important dates for individuals

Can you help if the CRA reviews or audits me?

Yes. We help you respond to CRA reviews, audits and reassessments and keep track of appeal deadlines. For most taxpayers, a Notice of Objection must be filed within 90 days of the notice date; individuals may instead have up to one year after the filing due date, whichever is later.

Source: CRA — P148 Resolving your dispute

When is a corporate (T2) return due?

A T2 corporation income tax return is generally due no later than six months after the end of the corporation's tax year. See our business tax services.
